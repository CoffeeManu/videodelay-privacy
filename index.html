Privacy Policy for VideoDelay

Last updated: September 30, 2026

VideoDelay is a sports video delay and recording application.

Camera Access

VideoDelay requires access to the device camera in order to display, delay and record video. Camera images are processed locally on the user's device.

Microphone Access

VideoDelay requires access to the microphone in order to record audio together with video recordings. Audio is processed locally on the user's device.

Storage and Recordings

Temporary video and audio data may be stored locally on the device as part of the video delay buffer. These temporary files are used only to provide the delayed-video functionality and are removed when they are no longer required by the application.

When the user chooses to record a video, the resulting video is saved locally to the device's media storage.

Data Collection and Sharing

VideoDelay does not transmit camera recordings, microphone recordings or delayed-video buffer data to the developer or to external servers.

VideoDelay does not use user accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking services.

Data Retention and Deletion

Temporary delay-buffer data is stored only locally and is removed when it is no longer required by the application.

Saved recordings remain on the user's device until the user deletes them.

Third-Party Services

VideoDelay is distributed through Google Play. Google may process information related to downloading, purchasing and using apps according to Google's own privacy policies.

Security

Video and audio processing performed by VideoDelay takes place locally on the user's device.

Children

VideoDelay is not specifically directed at children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the functionality of VideoDelay changes. The current version will always be available on this page.
